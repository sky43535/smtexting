2Your Messages & SkyCipher Encryption

Nuvira protects your conversations with SkyCipher, our end-to-end encryption framework.

  • The content of your messages, photos, lists, reactions and similar chat content is encrypted on your device before it is sent, and can only be decrypted by the people in the conversation. We cannot read it.
  • Your private encryption keys are created and kept on your device, protected by the Secure Enclave, and are never sent to us.
  • Your public key is stored with your profile so others can start encrypted chats with you.
  • To deliver messages we handle limited information that isn't message content, such as who a message is from and to, when it was sent, and its type (for example "text" or "image").
  • Deleting: you can delete your side of a conversation at any time. Once both people in a chat have deleted it, the messages are permanently removed from our servers. "Delete for everyone" removes a message for both people.

3The Nuvira Trust Authority

The Nuvira Trust Authority helps keep Nuvira genuine and safe. It checks that you're using the real Nuvira app, that your account is in good standing, and that the encryption keys people chat with really belong to them.

What your device sends

  • Proof that the app is genuine, made with Apple's App Attest service using a key that belongs to that install of the app. This key is not linked to your account.
  • Your account sign-in, so passes are issued to you.
  • The public half of your encryption keys, to create your chat certificate. The private half never leaves your device.
  • Your app version, so we can let you know when an update is required.

What's stored on your device

A few signed passes (access, account status, chat certificate and request signing). Each contains your account ID, what it allows, when it was issued, when it expires and our signature. They're kept in protected storage that is locked while your device is locked and is not backed up to iCloud, and they renew automatically.

What we keep

The app install key (not linked to you), a fingerprint of your public key so the people you message can verify it, and your account status. Never your messages or private keys.

If a check can't be completed, or something looks wrong, Nuvira may stay locked until it can be verified. This protects you and the people you talk to.

  • Subscription status: whether you have Nuvira Plus or Pro, verified with Apple. We never receive your payment details.
  • Location: only when you choose to share a place or map pin in a chat, and that content is encrypted like other messages.
